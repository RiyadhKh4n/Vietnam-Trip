⚠ Itinerary — Key Changes v5
Ha Giang cut to a 3-day / 2-night loop, reached by the Saturday-night sleeper bus. You keep your full day in Hanoi on Sat 7 Nov, board the overnight bus ~21:00, and start riding at dawn on Sun 8 Nov — no daytime transfer day burned. Road Kings Day 1 Du Gia (Sun), Day 2 Ma Pi Leng / Nho Que / Lung Cu → Dong Van (Mon), Day 3 finish Ha Giang City ~15:00 Tue 10 Nov, van back to the Sofitel.

The 2 reclaimed nights went to Hoi An and HCMC. Hoi An is now 3 nights (Sat 14 – Mon 16) with a proper measure → fit → collect cycle and a My Son / beach flex day. HCMC is now 4 nights + the final night, and the Ben Tre / My Tho Mekong day is back in (Sat 21). Phu Quoc stays a full 5 nights.

Everything between shifts ~2 days earlier. Hanoi return/rest day Wed 11 Nov · Lan Ha Bay cruise Thu–Fri 12–13 · Hoi An 14–16 · Dalat 16–18 · HCMC 18–21. The Phu Quoc block and the fixed Qatar flights are untouched.

Order recap: Hanoi (2 days) → Ha Giang (night bus in, 3d/2n) → Hanoi (rest day) → Lan Ha Bay → Hoi An → Dalat → HCMC → Phu Quoc.

Carried over from v4: the loop returns to the Sofitel Metropole (no Pullman airport hotel, no bag courier) · the 30kg cases stay stored at the Sofitel across both Hanoi stays, collected Fri 13 Nov after the cruise · Dalat stays 2 nights (standalone sightseeing day cut, per plan).

Hoi An flood risk unchanged. November 2025 flooded the Old Town 2–8 Nov and 15–19 Nov — overlapping these dates. Hanoi leather shops are the primary commission route; My Son Sanctuary is the wet-day full-day contingency.

PQC → SGN must be the 07:00–08:00 departure. Qatar T2 check-in closes ~16:40 on Sat 28 Nov; 15:00 landing leaves only ~100 min for T1→T2 transfer, which is insufficient. Non-negotiable.

🏨
Anantara Hoi An Resort — River Suite
Steps from all tailors and leather shops · 3 nights · Book direct or Amex Travel
⚠ November 2025 saw Old Town flood 2–8 Nov and again 15–19 Nov — overlapping these travel dates. During active flood events Yaly, A Dong Silk, and riverside leather shops are inaccessible on foot. The Japanese Covered Bridge was submerged near its roof. Primary mitigation: commission Hanoi leather (Lanh Leather, HuongCraft) during your Hanoi days — treat Hoi An leather as a bonus not the mission. Secondary: book tailor appointments on higher ground — Tran Hung Dao and Le Loi streets flood far less than the riverside Old Town. If Old Town is flooded: My Son Sanctuary (40km, 4th–14th century Cham temple complex in jungle, fully weather-resilient) is the best full-day alternative. Tailoring window is now a generous 3 days — Sat 14 Nov measurements, Sun 15 Nov first fitting / My Son contingency, Mon 16 Nov final fittings + collection.
