⚠ Itinerary — Key Changes v6
No more Hanoi backtrack — overnight bus straight from Ha Giang to Ha Long Bay. The loop finishes ~15:00 in Ha Giang City on Tue 10 Nov and you board a second overnight sleeper bus directly to Ha Long Bay, arriving ~dawn Wed 11. The Hanoi return/rest day is gone entirely.

Ha Long Bay is now a 2-night cruise (Wed 11 – Fri 13). The extra cruise night absorbs the two Hanoi nights you dropped, so nothing from Hoi An onward moves — Hoi An still arrives Fri 13, Dalat 16–18, HCMC 18–21, Phu Quoc 22–26, home Sat 28.

You carry the luggage — no Sofitel storage. Both 30kg cases travel with you: on the Sat-night bus to Ha Giang, left with Road Kings at their base during the ride, collected at the finish, then onward by bus to Ha Long and by air to Da Nang. The Sofitel is now a single arrival night (Nov 6).

Fly to Hoi An from Hai Phong, not Hanoi. After the cruise you transfer to Hai Phong (Cat Bi) airport and fly HPH → Da Nang — Hanoi is never on the map again after Day 2.

Ha Giang is a 3-day / 2-night Road Kings loop reached by the Sat-night sleeper bus (start riding at dawn Sun 8). Du Gia (Sun) · Ma Pi Leng / Nho Que / Lung Cu → Dong Van (Mon) · Tham Ma / Quan Ba → finish Tue 10.

Order recap: Hanoi (2 days) → Ha Giang (night bus in, 3d/2n) → Ha Long Bay (night bus in, 2-night cruise) → Hoi An → Dalat → HCMC → Phu Quoc.

Leather note: since you don't return to Hanoi, anything commissioned at Lanh Leather / HuongCraft on Nov 6–7 must be shipped; Hoi An and HCMC (Koi Leather) are the in-person routes. Hoi An flood risk unchanged (Old Town flooded 2–8 & 15–19 Nov in 2025); My Son Sanctuary is the wet-day contingency.

PQC → SGN must be the 07:00–08:00 departure. Qatar T2 check-in closes ~16:40 on Sat 28 Nov; 15:00 landing leaves only ~100 min for T1→T2 transfer, which is insufficient. Non-negotiable.

Buy Viettel or Mobifone SIM at Noi Bai arrivals on 6 Nov. Unlimited data ~£5–8. Do not rely on roaming. Download Ha Giang offline maps before boarding the Sat 7 Nov night bus.
Pre-trip
Luggage on the move: the 30kg cases ride the sleeper buses (in the hold) and the cruise (in-cabin) — pack them so they're lockable and haul-able on/off buses. Confirm Road Kings storage during the loop and the cruise's in-cabin storage.
Pre-trip
Leather commission check-in — set WhatsApp reminder mid-October (3 weeks before) to request photo updates from all leather shops. Catches problems before arrival.
Pre-trip
Amex FHR bookings must be made through Amex Travel portal, not directly with hotels. Confirm FHR inventory available for Sofitel Metropole and Dalat Palace — peak season allocation sells out.
Pre-trip
Departure luggage: by 27 Nov you will have 30kg bags plus leather commissions plus tailored garments. Factor volume into T1→T2 transfer at Tan Son Nhat and check Qatar's additional bag policy.
